The short version

  • Your results stay on your PC unless you share them. Ping history, speed tests, incidents, your device list and your settings are stored on your PC. We don't have a copy.
  • Two things are sent automatically, and you can turn off both:
    1. Crash reports. If HonestPing closes unexpectedly, it sends us a short report the next time it starts, so we can fix the problem. Personal and network details are removed first. (In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and Canada, or if your region is unknown, both happen only if you allow them.)
    2. Provider name lookup. To show your provider, HonestPing asks a public lookup service, which sees your public IP address. We don't receive it.
  • Everything else is sent only when you choose: bug reports, feature requests, and joining the waitlist.
  • We don't sell your personal data, show ads, or use advertising trackers.
  • You can ask us to see or delete what we hold. Write to hello@honestping.com.

3. The HonestPing app

3.1 What stays on your PC

HonestPing stores these only on your PC, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\PingTray:

  • ping history, incidents, daily recaps, speed-test and load-test results, and 7-day reports
  • your settings, including your Wi-Fi network name (used only to know when you switch networks), custom monitor targets, trial dates, and your share code
  • the device list from Devices → Scan, which is kept in memory only and never saved or sent
  • a local crash file, which is deleted after it's sent, after one failed retry, or when you turn crash reports off (see 3.2)

We can't see any of this. Uninstalling HonestPing leaves your history in place so a reinstall keeps it. Delete the folder above to remove it.

Network checks. To tell whether a problem is on your side or further out, HonestPing sends small test messages (pings) to your router and to public servers run by Cloudflare (1.1.1.1), Google (8.8.8.8, google.com) and Quad9 (9.9.9.9), and it checks whether services you choose to monitor answer. These checks don't contain your personal data, but like any internet connection, the servers they reach can see your public IP address. Speed tests and load tests, which you start yourself, download test data from Cloudflare (falling back to OVH) and upload made-up test data, not your files, to Cloudflare (falling back to httpbin.org).

Update check. When you press Check for updates, HonestPing reads a small file from honestping.com. That request includes your IP address and app version, like any web request. [Confirm it stays manual-only.]

LAN compare. LAN compare is off until you turn it on. Shares a small status snapshot with other HonestPing PCs on your home network. No addresses or history.

3.2 Automatic crash reports

What happens. If HonestPing closes unexpectedly, it sends us a short report the next time it starts, so we can fix the problem. Personal and network details are removed first. If sending fails, it tries once more on the next launch. Then the file is deleted either way.

Your choice. Crash reports are controlled by Settings → Privacy → Send crash reports automatically. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy.

  • US and most other regions: on by default. You can turn it off at any time. When you do, HonestPing shows "Crash reports are off. Any report waiting to be sent was deleted." and nothing is sent after that.
  • The EEA, the UK, Switzerland and Canada, or if your region is unknown: Crash reports and provider name lookup happen only if you allow them. HonestPing asks the first time it starts. HonestPing decides this on your PC from your Windows region setting. It doesn't look up your location. Never included: your files, history, test results, settings, Wi-Fi names, IP addresses, device names, Windows username or provider name.

Your IP address reaches our server like any connection. We don't store it.

Raw reports are deleted within 90 days.

3.4 Provider name lookup

To show your provider, HonestPing asks a public lookup service, which sees your public IP address. We don't receive it. In order, HonestPing tries api.ipify.org, Cloudflare (1.1.1.1/cdn-cgi/trace) and icanhazip.com for your public IP address, then ipapi.co and ipwho.is for the provider name. These services receive your public IP address and handle it under their own privacy policies. HonestPing doesn't show your IP address, save it, or send it to us. On by default in the US and most other regions. In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and Canada, or if your region is unknown, it runs only if you allow it. Turn it off with Settings → Privacy → Show my provider's name (or set Isp=0 in settings.txt), and it stops right away.

9. Your choices and rights

In the app: turn off crash reports, provider name lookup and LAN compare in Settings → Privacy. Delete local data by removing %LOCALAPPDATA%\PingTray.

By email: write to hello@honestping.com to ask what we hold about you, get a copy, correct it, delete it, or object to how we use it. We'll answer within 45 days (one month in the EU and UK) and may need to confirm it's you, usually by replying from the same email address. Many crash reports have no name, email or ID, so we may not be able to find yours. If you tell us roughly when it was sent, we'll try. We'll delete everything else and tell you what we kept and why.

US state rights. Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Virginia, Oregon or Texas), you may have the right to know, access, correct, delete and port your data, and to opt out of the sale of personal data, targeted advertising and certain profiling. We don't sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising or profiling, so there's nothing to opt out of. If we ever do, we'll ask first and honor Global Privacy Control signals. You can use an authorized agent. If we deny a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer with "Appeal." If you're in Colorado and disagree with the appeal decision, you can contact the Colorado Attorney General. We won't treat you differently for using your rights. 10. Children

HonestPing isn't directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EU/UK), and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you think a child has sent us information, email us and we'll delete it.

11. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) for everything HonestPing sends, private storage for attachments, access limited to HonestPing's owner with two-factor sign-in. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we'll notify you as the law requires.
