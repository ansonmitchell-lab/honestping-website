Full bars · no usable path

When the bars look fine and nothing works

Signal strength is not the same as a working internet path. HonestPing checks several independent internet services and separates “Wi-Fi is up” from “the internet is answering.” You stop guessing whether to reboot the laptop, the router, or wait it out.

You get: a plain verdict plus the checks that back it up.

Most people are stuck between three incomplete options: the Wi-Fi icon on the taskbar, a one-time speed test that shows a single moment, or heavy monitoring built for IT desks, not for someone trying to finish a video call at home. What you already have
