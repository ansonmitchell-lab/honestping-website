Are you getting what you pay for?

Your plan says one speed. HonestPing tells you the real one.

Type in the download speed your provider advertises. HonestPing tests your real connection over time, compares the results to that promise, and gives you a straight answer: you’re getting what you pay for, or you’re not.

  1. 01

    Enter your advertised speed

    Type the download speed on your plan or bill, like 500 Mbps. That’s the promise we hold your provider to.

  2. 02

    Test it for real, over time

    Pro runs three short tests a day for 7 days, morning to evening plus a weekend day, while HonestPing is open. Not one lucky test at 3 a.m.

  3. 03

    Compare to the promise

    Every result is measured against what you were sold, so slow evenings and chronic under-delivery are impossible to miss.

  4. 04

    Get a clear verdict

    Getting what you pay for, Close to plan, or Under-delivering. Plain words, with dated results you can copy or export.

A dated record to bring to your provider

Let HonestPing build your case before disputing your bill or switching ISPs. Have the proof that you need. When you call your provider, you won’t be arguing from a feeling.

What it does for you

Answers you can act on, not numbers to stare at

When a call stutters or a stream stalls, you need to know if it’s your Wi-Fi, your router, or the wider internet. HonestPing checks whether the internet is actually answering and tells you what it found, in language you’d say to a friend.

Full bars · no usable path

When the bars look fine and nothing works

Signal strength is not the same as a working internet path. HonestPing checks several independent internet services and separates “Wi-Fi is up” from “the internet is answering.” You stop guessing whether to reboot the laptop, the router, or wait it out.

You get: a plain verdict plus the checks that back it up.

The gap in the market

Built for the space in between

Most people are stuck between three incomplete options: the Wi-Fi icon on the taskbar, a one-time speed test that shows a single moment, or heavy monitoring built for IT desks, not for someone trying to finish a video call at home. HonestPing fills that middle: consumer-friendly, continuously honest, and explained in plain language.

What you already have

Radio bars & “Connected”

Show signal to the access point, not whether packets reach the internet. Full bars can still mean the internet isn’t answering: a sign-in page, a name-lookup failure, or an outage past your router.

What people search for

One-time speed tests

A single number from a single moment. Silent about jitter, dropouts, and whether your connection was healthy five minutes ago.
