About HonestPing

Network health, explained

HonestPing is an app for two questions: is my internet working right now, and am I getting the speed I pay for? It checks whether the internet is answering, then says what it found, with ping, jitter, packet loss, and recent history beside the verdict.

Wi-Fi bars show signal, not a working path. A speed test shows one moment. IT monitors are built for desks, not a living room. HonestPing sits in the middle: clear, continuous, and honest.
