About HonestPing

Network health, explained

HonestPing is a Windows app that answers two questions: is my internet working right now, and am I getting the speed I pay for? It checks whether the internet is actually answering, then tells you what it found in plain language, with the evidence (ping, jitter, packet loss) and recent history right beside the verdict.

We built it because the options people actually have are incomplete. Wi-Fi bars show signal, not a working path. One-time speed tests show a single number from a single moment. Enterprise monitors are built for IT desks. HonestPing sits in the middle: clear, continuous, and honest.
