The problem, in one line

When a customer says “my internet is slow,” nobody on the call can see what they’re seeing. Is it their Wi-Fi? A busy laptop? Your network? Every minute spent guessing is support time you pay for. HonestPing is being built to answer that before the call, or during it, with the same data on both sides.

Benefits

Fewer calls, shorter calls, better answers

Cut calls about home Wi-Fi.

A lot of “slow internet” is really weak Wi-Fi, a busy PC, or another device downloading. HonestPing tells your customer that directly, in plain words, with the fix. Many of those calls never need to happen, and every call that doesn’t happen is support time you don’t spend.

Shorter calls and fewer escalations when it is the network.

In development

A shared report will show when the trouble started, how long it lasted, and where it happened: on the PC, on the home Wi-Fi, at the router, or past it. Your team starts from evidence instead of a script, so the first person on the call can route it right.

Fewer complaints about speed.

Customers can see their real speed against their plan, measured over time, not from one test. If your service is good, they’ll see that for themselves, and there’s less to dispute. When performance is good, customers see they’re getting 100% of the value they pay for, the same whether or not their provider is a partner.

Early warning for your network team.

In development

When many customers in one area see the same spike in loss or delay, your team will get an alert by email, team chat, or webhook, so you can start on the problem before calls are coming in. Area views will only ever show grouped, anonymous data.

How it will work in the pilot In development

Same report on both ends of the call

  1. 01

    Your customers use HonestPing.

    It sits in the Windows tray and watches their connection: live ping, jitter and loss, a simple green, amber or red status, and short speed tests three times a day for a week, compared with the plan they entered. In the pilot, your customers get Pro included.

  2. 02

    They choose what to share.

    Customers will be able to turn on anonymous contributions, which helps you see area health. Nothing will be sent unless they switch it on. You won’t be able to switch it on for them.

  3. 03

    When they need help, they’ll send you a share code.

    In the app they’ll click Share with my provider and get a short code. They can read it to your agent or paste it into a chat or ticket. The code will expire, and they can cancel it at any time.

  4. 04

    Your team will see the same report they do.

    Your agent will enter the code in the HonestPing portal and see the customer’s report, explained in the agent’s language. Your network team will see area health, trends, advertised versus real speed, and alerts.

Our privacy promise

We wrote these first and are building the program around them

  • Nobody can edit or hide the measurements. Each shared report will carry a fingerprint both sides can check, so nobody, us included, can quietly change the numbers. Your notes, like “planned maintenance,” will always appear as your notes, separate from the data.
  • Sharing will be opt-in. Off by default, for every customer, including customers who get Pro through you.
  • Individual reports will need the customer’s OK. You’ll see one customer’s details only when they give you a share code. They’ll see every time it’s opened.
  • Area data will be grouped and anonymous. Areas will be approximate and show results only when enough different people contributed. There’s no way to drill down to a household.
  • No precise locations, no device lists, no Wi-Fi names. Your area will be worked out roughly from the connection, and the address used to do that thrown away right after.
  • We don’t sell data, run ads, or use outside trackers.
  • Same verdicts for everyone. Working with us never changes what the app tells your customers. If the problem is on your network, the app says so.

FAQ

Questions providers ask

Is this a tool for customers to catch us out?

No. It’s a tool for seeing what’s really happening. When service is good, customers see that. When something’s wrong, you find out where, faster. We call it a confidence tool, and we built it so neither side can bend the numbers.

Can we change or remove results we disagree with?

No. Nobody will be able to, including us. You’ll be able to add notes to explain context, like maintenance windows, and they’ll show as your notes. If you think a measurement is wrong, tell us. If there’s a problem with how we measure, we fix the method for everyone.

What data do we actually get?

In the pilot, you’ll get grouped, anonymous area health from customers who opted in: delay, jitter, loss, outage reports, and speed compared with plans, by approximate area and time of day. Individual customer reports will come only when that customer shares a code with you.

Can we turn on data sharing for our customers?

No. Each customer decides for themselves. Including Pro in your plans doesn’t change that.

Does partnering change what the app tells our customers?

No. The app gives every customer the same honest verdict, whether or not their provider works with us.

How accurate are the speed tests?

Each test is a short download and upload from the customer’s PC, three times a day for a week. Tests are left out when a VPN is on, Wi-Fi is weak, or the PC is busy. Very fast lines can read a little low today. A multi-connection method is in development, and we’ll publish the method.

What about customers on phones or Macs?

HonestPing is being built for Windows. Area health still works with the customers who use it. We’ll say clearly if that changes. We won’t promise it before it exists.

What languages are supported?

We’ll start with a few and add more based on what pilot partners need. The data is the same in every language. Only the explanations are translated.

How do outage alerts work?

When many customers in one area report trouble at the same time, your team will get an alert by email, team chat, or webhook. Slowdowns will show up quickly. A full outage will be confirmed as customers come back online, since a disconnected PC can’t report until it reconnects. We’re upfront about that.

Do we need our own network number to join?

No. Many smaller providers route through an upstream provider. You’ll be able to verify your own address ranges instead.

What does it cost?

We haven’t set partner pricing yet. Talk to us about your service and we’ll work it out with you before anything is agreed in writing.
