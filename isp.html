For internet providers

Cut the “my internet is slow” calls. Give real answers.

Built to cut complaints and support calls, and shorten the ones that remain. HonestPing is a Windows app that shows people how their internet is really doing: live ping, jitter, loss, and real speed compared with their plan. The problem, in one line

When a customer says “my internet is slow,” nobody on the call can see what they’re seeing. Is it their Wi-Fi? A busy laptop? Your network? Every minute spent guessing is support time you pay for. HonestPing is being built to answer that before the call, or during it, with the same data on both sides.

Benefits

Fewer calls, shorter calls, better answers

Cut calls about home Wi-Fi.

A lot of “slow internet” is really weak Wi-Fi, a busy PC, or another device downloading. HonestPing tells your customer that directly, in plain words, with the fix. Many of those calls never need to happen, and every call that doesn’t happen is support time you don’t spend.

Shorter calls and fewer escalations when it is the network.

In development

A shared report will show when the trouble started, how long it lasted, and where it happened: on the PC, on the home Wi-Fi, at the router, or past it. Your team starts from evidence instead of a script, and Claim Check will say how well it’s backed up, so the first person on the call can route it right.

Fewer complaints about speed.

Customers can see their real speed against their plan, measured over time, not from one test. If your service is good, they’ll see that for themselves, and there’s less to dispute. When performance is good, customers see they’re getting 100% of the value they pay for, the same whether or not their provider is a partner.

Early warning for your network team.

In development

How it will work in the pilot In development

Same report on both ends of the call

  1. 01

    Your customers use HonestPing.

    It sits in the Windows tray and watches their connection: live ping, jitter and loss, a simple green, amber or red status, and short speed tests three times a day for a week, compared with the plan they entered. In the pilot, your customers get Pro included.

  2. 02

    They choose what to share.

    Customers will be able to turn on anonymous contributions, which helps you see area health. Nothing will be sent unless they switch it on. You won’t be able to switch it on for them.

  3. 03

    When they need help, they’ll send you a share code.

    In the app they’ll click Share with my provider and get a short code for their 7-day report or speed test. They can read it to your agent or paste it into a chat or ticket. The code will expire, and they can cancel it at any time.

  4. 04

    Your team will see the same report they do.

    Your agent will enter the code in the HonestPing portal and see the customer’s report, explained in the agent’s language, with its Claim Check result. Your network team will see area health, trends, advertised versus real speed, alerts, and a monthly report, and will be able to post status updates. See the portal.

Our privacy promise

We wrote these first and are building the program around them

  • Nobody can edit or hide the measurements. Each shared report will carry a fingerprint both sides can check (see Claim Check), so nobody, us included, can quietly change the numbers. Your notes, like “planned maintenance,” and your status posts will always appear as yours, separate from the data.
  • Sharing will be opt-in. Off by default, for every customer, including customers who get Pro through you.
  • Individual reports will need the customer’s OK. You’ll see one customer’s details only when they give you a share code. They’ll see every time it’s opened.
  • Area data will be grouped and anonymous. Areas will be approximate and show results only when enough different people contributed. There’s no way to drill down to a household.
  • No precise locations, no device lists, no Wi-Fi names. Your area will be worked out roughly from the connection, and the address used to do that thrown away right after.
  • We don’t sell data, run ads, or use outside trackers.
  • No paid placement. You won’t be able to buy ranking positions, in-app ads, or messages that look like diagnostics.
  • Same verdicts for everyone. Working with us never changes what the app tells your customers. If the problem is on your network, the app says so.

Claim Check In development

How well is this report backed up?

When a customer shares a 7-day report or a speed test, Claim Check will tell your team how well it’s backed up by other evidence: one evidence level, up to three plain reasons, and one suggested next step. Support stops arguing over one screenshot, and your network team goes straight to the right area or the right house.

It will never say whether a customer is honest. It says where the evidence points, with the same logic and the same levels for every provider, partner or not.

Four evidence levels (working names)

Backed by area data

Grouped neighbors or your own logs show the same problem in the same hours.

Next step: Check the area’s capacity.

Does not mean a credit is owed. Any credit or offer stays your call, in your own billing.

Isolated, worth a tech check

A clean report, slow past the router, but not seen in area data. Neighbors look fine, or there are too few to compare.

Next step: A line check or tech visit for this home.

Does not mean the report is doubtful. Quiet areas land here too.

Points to home network

Most slow tests trace to Wi-Fi, the PC, a VPN, or the router.

Next step: Share the home fixes HonestPing already suggested.

Does not mean the customer is wrong about being slow.

Can’t verify yet

The report itself is thin: too few counted tests, an outdated app version, not tested on your network, or a copy that doesn’t match the original.

Next step: Ask the customer to finish the week or share again by code.

Does not mean the report is false. It just needs more to go on.

There will be no red level and no accusing wording. Missing neighbor data will never lower a level.

Five checks behind every level

  1. Report integrity. Each test will be signed where it runs, and the report’s fingerprint has to match HonestPing’s copy, so an edited copy simply fails. It will also confirm the test ran on your network, without passing along the customer’s IP address. A report shared again will show as shared before, not as a new claim.
  2. Home-side causes, ruled in or out. Wi-Fi or wired, a busy PC, a VPN, a router or port that can’t carry the plan, and a busy test server. Tests with a home-side cause will be marked not counted and never count against the customer or you.
  3. Corroboration. Grouped, anonymous results from opted-in neighbors on your network in the same hours, the customer’s own history, and your outage and maintenance logs if you connect them.
  4. Plausibility. Share of plan using the median, whether dips repeat across the 7 days, and outliers, like results faster than the home link allows, dropped and listed.
  5. One result. One evidence level, up to three reasons, and one next step your team can act on.

Fair to both sides

  • No accusations. Claim Check will judge evidence, never honesty.
  • Sparse areas are never penalized. A clean report in a quiet area will still land on a tech check, the same next step a busy area gets.
  • Neighbor data stays grouped and anonymous. It will show only above a minimum group size, never a single household, and households will opt in separately.
  • Opt-in per report. The customer will choose to share each report, every time, and can cancel a code. You’ll see only what they approved: no IP address, device names, Wi-Fi names, or browsing.
  • The customer will see the same level you see.
  • Hard to game. Several PCs in one home will count once. A sudden burst of new installs in one area will be held back and reviewed, and will never show as “Backed.” Rate limits and a server-side record of every test will make faked results costly and visible.

FAQ

Questions providers ask

Is this a tool for customers to catch us out?

No. It’s a tool for seeing what’s really happening. When service is good, customers see that. When something’s wrong, you find out where, faster. We call it a confidence tool, and we built it so neither side can bend the numbers.

Can we change or remove results we disagree with?

No. Nobody will be able to, including us. You’ll be able to add notes to explain context, like maintenance windows, and they’ll show as your notes. If you think a measurement is wrong, tell us. If there’s a problem with how we measure, we fix the method for everyone.

What data do we actually get?

In the pilot, you’ll get grouped, anonymous area health from customers who opted in: delay, jitter, loss, outage reports, and speed compared with plans, by approximate area and time of day. Individual customer reports, with their Claim Check result, will come only when that customer shares a code with you.

Can we turn on data sharing for our customers?

No. Each customer decides for themselves. Including Pro in your plans doesn’t change that.

Does partnering change what the app tells our customers?

No. The app gives every customer the same honest verdict, whether or not their provider works with us.

Will Claim Check tell us a customer is lying?

No. It will never judge honesty, and it has no red level. It will say where the evidence points, why, and what to do next. If evidence is missing, the level won’t drop because of it. Credits and offers stay your call.

Can a group of customers game Claim Check?

It’s being built to resist that. Several PCs in one home will count as one, sudden bursts of new installs in an area will be held back for review and never show as backed by area data, and every test will have a matching record on HonestPing’s test server.

How accurate are the speed tests?

Each test is a short download and upload from the customer’s PC, three times a day for a week. Tests are left out when a VPN is on, Wi-Fi is weak, or the PC is busy. Very fast lines can read a little low today. A multi-connection method is in development, and we’ll publish the method.

What about customers on phones or Macs?

HonestPing is being built for Windows. Area health still works with the customers who use it. We’ll say clearly if that changes. We won’t promise it before it exists.

What languages are supported?

We’ll start with a few and add more based on what pilot partners need. The data is the same in every language. Only the explanations are translated.

How do outage alerts work?

When many customers in one area report trouble at the same time, your team will get an alert by email, team chat, or webhook. Slowdowns will show up quickly. A full outage will be confirmed as customers come back online, since a disconnected PC can’t report until it reconnects. We’re upfront about that.

Do we need our own network number to join?

No. Many smaller providers route through an upstream provider. You’ll be able to verify your own address ranges instead.

What does it cost?

We haven’t set partner pricing yet. Talk to us about your service and we’ll work it out with you before anything is agreed in writing.

Who’s behind HonestPing?
