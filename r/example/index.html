How the tests were run Method · connection · overpay formula

Each test

  • Multi-connection: 4 parallel download connections (8 on lines over 300 Mbps), 2 for upload.
  • Warm-up skipped: the first 1.0 s of each transfer isn't counted, so connection start-up doesn't drag the number down.
  • Median: the result is the median of 250 ms steps across the rest of the transfer.
  • Length: automatic tests: 4 s down, 3 s up. Quick checks: 10 s down, 8 s up. Same method version (v2) for all 19 tests.
  • Ping: median of 10 idle pings, plus ping measured during the download.

The week

  • 3 slots a day: morning (7 to 10:30 AM), afternoon (12 to 4 PM), evening (5:30 to 9:30 PM), shifted a little each day.
  • Missed slots aren't made up in a burst. A quick check in an empty slot fills it (test #8).
  • Percent of plan uses the plan saved when the week started: 100 Mbps down, 20 Mbps up.
  • Test server: public speed-test server, Denver area. All times Mountain Time (UTC−6).
